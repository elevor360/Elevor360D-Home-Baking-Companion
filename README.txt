Elevor360D Home Baking Companion V1
=================================
50 distinct baking recipes; large graphic cooking timers; baking conversions;
favourites, meal planner, shopping list, personal recipes, backups.
Universal Advertising Manager-compatible advertising/adverts.json and images folder.

UPLOAD TO GITHUB:
Upload index.html, app.js, recipes.json, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, icon.svg,
and the advertising/ folder to the root of your public GitHub Pages repository.
Keep Elevor360D_Baking_Recipe_Manager_V1.html on your computer (DO NOT publish).
Open the manager, import recipes.json, make edits, download new recipes.json and
replace the one in GitHub. After updating recipes, refresh the installed app online.
For offline updates, service-worker cache may need a version increment in sw.js.

ADVERTS:
Existing advertising/adverts.json and advertising/images/Sunset Banner.png
are included from the working Recipe Book package. Update using your Universal
Advertising Manager if its output format is compatible.

SAFETY:
Recipe methods are editorial drafts, not kitchen-tested. Check allergens, oven
temperatures, baking results and safe food handling before public publication.
